Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough. No online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission replay — keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter.

  1. 1 Play the story

    Chapters 1 to 4 plus the finale. story trophies pop on their own — except one thing: do the balcony meditation with Lucia at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, after Down to Earth. It is the only possibly missable trophy.

  2. 2 Reach 100% completion

    Open Progress → Stats → 100% Completion after the story and clean up only what is left: robberies, fencing, collectibles, races, 42 Shooting Range golds, 42 drone trials, 300 stunts, 70 challenges. trophies live here.

  3. 3 Strangers & clean-up

    Finish eight Stranger mission strands, including the final missions of Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone. trophies live here.

  4. 4 The platinum

    Vice Got a Price! pops the moment the other 54 are done. Roughly 200 hours in total, most of it on the 100% checklist.

⚠ 1 possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots. One spot is the Chapter 1 balcony meditation with Lucia; miss it and you may need a new playthrough.
